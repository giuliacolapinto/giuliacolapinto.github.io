Trained, validated, and tuned multiple classification models (e.g., Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machine, and Random Forest), evaluating them across advanced metrics such as Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC curves, achieving an outstanding predictive accuracy of over 95% on the test set.
