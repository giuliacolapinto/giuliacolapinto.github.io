About Me

I am the union of two things: sports and mathematics. My father put me in the pool when I was just 3 months old, and I have practiced sports — ranging from swimming to weightlifting — my entire life.

My passion for math, on the other hand, started at age 8 with Sudoku puzzles, which I used to solve while watching TV because they relaxed me. The discipline I learned from sports combined with my love for logic puzzles perfectly translates into my passion for statistics: you keep banging your head against a problem until you find the best possible solution (or the best model!).
